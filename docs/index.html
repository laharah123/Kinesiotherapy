Kinesiotherapy

Guided exercise routines for back, neck, shoulder, hip, knee and wrist complaints, adapted to how you feel.

Tell the app where it hurts, answer a few questions, and it builds a four week routine of gentle, timed exercises with animated guidance. After each exercise you rate your pain, and the next session adjusts. Free for 7 days, then a monthly or yearly subscription.

Kinesiotherapy is a self-guided exercise tool, not medical care. Please read the health guidance in our Terms of Service before starting.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription? In your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are issued by Apple or Google. On iOS go to reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android use the Google Play order history.

How do I delete my account and data? Email us with the subject "Delete my account" from the address on your account. Everything is removed from our database immediately and from backups within 30 days.

I subscribed but the app still shows the trial ended. Open Profile and tap Restore purchases. If that does not help, email us with the email address on your account and the platform you used.

Something hurts during an exercise. Stop. Mark it as "Pinched" or "Severe" in the check-in so the app removes or eases it, and see a clinician if the pain persists.
