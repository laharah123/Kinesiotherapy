2. What the app is, and what it is not

The app offers guided exercise routines for common musculoskeletal complaints and adapts them to the pain ratings you enter. It is a self-guided exercise tool. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, and nothing in it is medical advice. See the Terms of Service for the full health disclaimer.

4. How we use it

  • To provide the service: sign you in, generate your plan, run sessions, and adjust difficulty based on your ratings.
  • To show you your own history and progress.
  • To manage your free trial and subscription.
  • To answer support requests you send us.
  • To keep the service secure and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling for marketing purposes, or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. The only automated processing is the exercise adaptation you can see in the app, which changes the number of repetitions, sets and rest time of your next session based on the ratings you gave.

5. Legal bases (EEA and UK users)

  • Contract: account data and subscription status, needed to provide the app you signed up for.
  • Consent: health related intake answers, pain ratings and notes.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure and answering support email.
  • Legal obligation: tax and accounting records related to purchases, held by the app stores.

10. Security

Data travels between the app and our servers over TLS. Each user can only read and write their own rows, enforced at the database level. Passwords are hashed. Subscription status is written only by our server after the app store confirms a purchase. No system is perfectly secure, so if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will notify you as the law requires.

11. Children

The app is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK). If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Data stored on your device

The app keeps a copy of your session token, plan and recent progress in the app's private storage so it works offline and opens quickly. Uninstalling the app removes this copy. It does not use cookies or third-party tracking technologies.
